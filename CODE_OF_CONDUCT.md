# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be welcoming and free of harassment
for everyone. That includes people of any age, body size, visible or invisible
disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, and sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Criticise ideas, code and decisions, not people. Explain your reasoning and
  stay open to other views.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assume good faith, and ask when something is unclear.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn
  from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people who use it.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment in any form, in public or in private.
- Personal attacks, insults, and derogatory or demeaning comments.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, and sexualised language or imagery.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing others' private information, such as contact details or
  information about their employer, without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, issues,
pull requests, reviews, discussions and any other project channel. It also
applies when someone represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

This project does not yet have a dedicated contact address for conduct
reports. Until one is listed here:

- Use GitHub's built-in reporting (**Report content** on a comment, issue or
  pull request, or **Block or report** on a user's profile) for content on
  GitHub.
- Do not report conduct issues in a public issue if doing so would expose the
  person reporting or the person affected.

If a report also involves a security vulnerability, follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for that part.

Maintainers will respect the privacy and security of anyone who reports an
incident.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They
may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that do not follow this Code of Conduct. Depending on the
severity and on any previous incidents, they may respond in one of these ways:

1. **Correction:** a private note explaining what was inappropriate, with a
   request to change it.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and a
   period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban:** a temporary ban from interacting with the project.
4. **Permanent ban:** for a pattern of violations, harassment, or aggression
   towards individuals or groups.
